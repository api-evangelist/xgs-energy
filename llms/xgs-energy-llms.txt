## How it behaves

- Pagination: page + per_page (1-100, default 10), plus X-WP-Total, X-WP-TotalPages and an RFC 8288 Link header.
- Field selection: _fields for sparse fieldsets, _embed to inline linked resources, context=view|embed.
- Errors: the WordPress {code, message, data.status} envelope. Not RFC 9457 problem+json.
- Rate limits: none published. No X-RateLimit-*, RateLimit-* or Retry-After header is returned.
- Caching: cache-control max-age=600, must-revalidate. WP Engine origin behind Cloudflare.
- Writes: none available anonymously. There is nothing to make idempotent and nothing to reverse.
